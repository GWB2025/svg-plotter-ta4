  1. Use the paper you intend to draw on. Choose line spacing no greater than the pen’s actual drawn line width so the patch has no white gaps. Start with 0.3 mm for your Sharpies; finer pens may need closer spacing. Cross-hatching adds a second ink pass and may make the colour darker.
  2. Let the ink dry fully. Calibrate ColorReader EZ in the Datacolor phone app, then place it flat on the centre of each uniform patch. Keep it clear of labels and edges. Inspect the patch for streaks or gaps before reading.
  3. Open the measured colour’s Info in the Datacolor app to see RGB or HEX. Use Enter RGB beside the matching numbered pen below to save those values to its ink colour. Read each patch individually.

The default 20 × 20 mm patch exceeds Datacolor’s recommended minimum uniform area of 6.35 × 6.35 mm. The result describes that ink on that paper at this fill density. Device readings are entered manually; SVG Plotter does not connect to ColorReader Bluetooth.

  1. Add the bottle here. Its RGB / HEX colour is your estimate or a reading from a dried swatch; record the paper used.
  2. In Pen stock, add or edit the refillable pen and select its Bottled ink. Choose Copy ink RGB to pen if you want to use the bottle’s colour as the pen’s starting colour.
  3. Use that pen’s Swatch button to prepare a patch, plot it and let it dry. Enter the ColorReader RGB in the pen editor. Keep a separate pen reading for each pen and paper combination you use.

Ink names appear in new drawing assignments and pen-change prompts. Editing a bottle does not overwrite a pen’s RGB reading or a loaded drawing. Marking a bottle unavailable leaves already-filled pens available. A bottle is recorded separately from the physical pen used to plot it.

Existing items are retained. Identical items are reused; conflicting pens, inks or themes are added as separate copies, with their ink links and pen assignments preserved. No drawing or recovery information is changed.

About SVG Plotter

Version {{APP_VERSION}} · macOS application

Prepare SVG artwork or plain text for a compatible GRBL Z-lift plotter, or export prepared SVG for other machines. The Writing Robot T-A4 remains the default profile.

  • SVG outlines and layered colour fills, with adjustable hatch spacing.
  • Saved plotter profiles; A3, A4, A5, A6, postcards, envelopes and custom sizes within the selected bed.
  • Text and ASCII art, postcard layouts, stroke fonts and private TrueType font import.
  • Personal pen and ink stock, editable colour themes, swatch sheets and catalogue backups.
  • Pen changes, parked pauses and recovery of interrupted drawings.

Your catalogue, profiles, fonts, saved postcard draft, recovery records and logs are kept on this Mac, separately from the application. Replacing the app preserves them. Opening the app does not connect to USB or move the plotter.
