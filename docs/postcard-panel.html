The pale printed-card reference and dashed boxes are preview guides only. The address starts near the four printed lines, 12 mm apart; adjust against your card after a trial. Select Draw centre divider only for a blank card. X and Y are measured from the card’s top-left home position.

    Save your draft to keep the text and settings for another session. Preparing a layout switches to Plot drawing for pen selection and the usual start checks; it does not move the plotter.

    Text sizes and placement

    Size is in typographic points (1 pt = 0.353 mm of the font’s em square); actual letter height varies with the font. Line spacing is the distance between baselines in millimetres. Y places the top of an ordinary capital letter; accents may extend above it. Width controls word wrapping. Enter inserts a new line, and blank lines are retained.

    Use Import fonts to add .ttf files (up to 2 MB each). They appear in both Font menus with an “outline” label and are saved separately from this draft. Keep the originals as a backup. The four bundled Hershey fonts are designed for pen strokes. Accents and common punctuation are supported; unavailable characters are reported. Save an overflowing draft if you want to refine it later, but correct the highlighted issues before exporting or plotting.
