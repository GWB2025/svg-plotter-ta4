Open the plotting window on your Mac

Create your handwriting on any supported device, then download its G-code and transfer the file to the Mac connected to the plotter.

  1. In the SVG Plotter folder on that Mac, double-click Start-SVG-Plotter.command.
  2. The local plotting window opens. Choose your G-code file and inspect the A4 preview.
  3. Connect the idle plotter, use Home, prepare the paper and start the drawing.

Once the launcher is running on this Mac, open the local plotting window. This address works only on the connected Mac; it will not launch the window from an iPad.

Getting started · connection help
  1. Connect the selected plotter directly to this Mac with a USB data cable, then choose Refresh connections.
  2. Select the plotter and choose Connect. Close other programs using its USB port if connection fails.
  3. Clear the bed and choose Home. Place your sheet at the top-left home position.
  4. Load the small-star test drawing, review the preview and pen, confirm readiness and choose Start drawing.

If no plotter appears, check its power, USB cable and any hub, then reconnect and refresh. Opening this app never starts movement.

Choose an example to try the preview and plotting controls.

For landscape SVG artwork, keep the physical page in portrait and choose Rotate 90° clockwise. Each click turns it another quarter turn; choose 0° to restore it. Rotation updates the preview and G-code together. Choose Keep original size and position for a 1:1 drawing, or Fit drawing to page to scale within the margins. Place the page at the bed’s top-left home position. Custom sizes must fit the selected plotter’s bed. SVG colours select the pen order. Between colours the robot lifts the pen, returns to the top-left position and waits for your confirmation. For overlapping filled artwork, choose Layered colour fills: only visible colours are hatched. White and very pale colours still hide lower layers when left blank. Hatch spacing is measured after placement; 0.5 mm suits the tested Sharpie pens. Fine details below 0.1 mm may be lost. For plain text and ASCII art, load a .txt file or open Text & ASCII art. SVG text must already be converted to paths. Review the preview before plotting.

Stock positioning guide · 210 × 297 mm

Choose Page size above, including Custom, then load a guide with four corner marks, four edge-centre ticks and a centre cross.

Plot onto a carrier sheet fixed to the bed, then place your paper, postcard or envelope against the corner marks. The top-left corner is home X0 Y0; envelopes use the narrow edge across the bed. Leave the carrier in place, keep the same page size and load your artwork. Loading a guide does not move the plotter.

Positioning guide loaded at exact size. Fitting, rotation, margins, hatching and themes are locked. Changing the page size regenerates the guide. Load artwork to restore the normal drawing controls.

Postcard text is loaded at exact size. Place the 148 × 105 mm card at the top-left home position. To revise the text, return to Postcard text and choose Prepare to plot again. The loaded drawing keeps the layout you last prepared.

    Recover after reset

    Reconnect USB. Remove the pen or lift its tip clear of the paper before continuing. Recovery clears the old movement queue, homes the plotter and returns with the holder raised. It then waits for you to fit the named pen and confirm Continue.

    Pause finishes queued movement, saves the stopping position, lifts the pen and parks at X0 Y0. Continue returns with the pen raised, restores its previous height and resumes. Keep the paper and pen unchanged. Cancel ends it and clears queued movement; use Home before starting again. After a dropped connection, the sender tries three times. If it still cannot reconnect, saved progress is retained. Reconnect and resume uses an unchanged controller position. Recover after reset re-establishes the origin, returns pen-up and asks for the saved pen before continuing. Completed stroke positions are saved to disk; an unfinished stroke may be retraced.

    Keep this Mac awake and the plotter connected. The sender continues if you close this browser tab; reopen it to control the job.

      Save the make, model and usable drawing area. Select SVG export for machines that use another controller or their own software.

      GRBL controller settings

      Use the values for your machine. Direct control requires GRBL status reports in millimetres (MPos and WCO), a Z-axis pen lift, homing to X0 Y0 at the top-left, and zero work offset. Servo M-codes, EBB and HP-GL controllers need SVG export. Saving a profile does not configure the controller.

      Leave both USB IDs blank to list USB serial devices. Check the selected port before connecting. A device without a serial number can only reconnect at its original port. Test custom profiles with the pen clear of the paper before drawing.
